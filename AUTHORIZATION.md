# Authorization

Yhwach is red-team automation for AI-security testing. It generates prompt injections, drives MCP/A2A abuse, enumerates AI infrastructure, and orchestrates post-exploitation. Every capability here is dual-use.

## Authorized use

- **OffSec OSAI certification exam.** The exam guide places no restriction on tooling. Yhwach is built to make that exam winnable at pace.
- **Authorized red-team engagements** with written scope you can produce.
- **Personal challenge labs** where you own the environment or hold a license (HackTheBox, TryHackMe, PortSwigger, offsec-hosted challenges, your own home lab).

## Not authorized

- Any system you do not own or lack written permission to test.
- Bug-bounty targets outside of a clearly published scope.
- Production AI systems, third-party APIs, or shared infrastructure without authorization from the operator of that system.

## Contributor rule

Playbook rules, actions, and example fixtures must be built against authorized targets. Do not submit fixtures containing real data, hostnames, or credentials from unauthorized engagements. Synthetic or lab-only data only.

## Responsibility

The user is responsible for lawful use. The author releases Yhwach as-is; misuse is the user's fault, not the tool's.
